How it works

  1. Set up the wall marks. Place two reference marks on a wall at a known distance apart, as the app shows during setup.
  2. Take the photo. Have your child stand against the wall and take the photo with the phone held upright.
  3. Confirm the lines. Check the marks and the head line the app found, adjust them if needed, then confirm to save the height estimate.

Approximate estimate, not a medical device. Results are for your own records.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the measurement approximate?
It is estimated from a photo, so camera angle, distance, posture, hair, and how well the lines are placed all affect it. Use the same spot and routine each time for the most consistent trend.
How do I restore my purchase?
Use the restore option in the app's settings. The app asks the App Store or Google Play whether your account owns the purchase. Use the same store account you bought with.
How do I delete my data?
Delete a single measurement, or all data for a child, inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes the data from your phone. We hold no copy of your photos or measurements, so there is nothing to delete on our side. The only thing the app can send is anonymous usage counts, which cannot be linked to you; you can turn them off in Settings.
What happens if I change phones?
Data lives on your phone. If your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) is on, it may be restored with that backup. Otherwise the new phone starts empty, and purchases can be restored as above.
Is there a subscription?
Yes, as one of three options. The 14-day free trial starts with your first measurement. After that, Height Count Pro is available as a 3-month subscription, a yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel them anytime in your device's Settings, under your Apple Account. The lifetime purchase is paid once and never renews.
