Height Count

Keep a record of how your child is growing. Take a photo against a wall with two reference marks, confirm the lines, and the app estimates height and saves it with the date.

Everything stays on your phone: no account, no ads, no tracking. A one-time purchase follows a 14-day free trial.

Approximate estimate, not a medical device. Heights are for your own records. For concerns about your child's health or growth, please consult a doctor.
