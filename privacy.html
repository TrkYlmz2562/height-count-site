Height Count Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

Height Count ("the app") helps parents keep a record of how their child is growing, using photos taken against a wall with two reference marks. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your photos and measurements never leave your phone, and the app sends only anonymous usage counts that you can switch off.

What we collect

Your photos, measurements, your child's name and birth date never leave your phone. We do not collect them.

The app does send anonymous usage counts to a server run by the developer (hosted on Cloudflare) when certain steps happen: first app launch, opening the app (once a day), adding the first child, starting a measurement, saving a measurement, seeing the purchase screen, completing a purchase, and coming back 1, 7, 14 and 30 days after installation. Each request contains only four things: the name of the event, the app version, the app language, and the platform (iOS or Android).

There is no device ID, no user ID, and no advertising ID. We do not store your IP address; it is used only for a moment to limit abuse. The server keeps only daily totals per event, which cannot be linked to any individual.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings ("Share anonymous usage counts", on by default). When it is off, nothing is sent.

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party analytics SDKs.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Photos you take or choose for a measurement.
  • Measurements (height estimates and dates), your child's name and optional birth date, and your settings (unit, reminder time, language).
  • A local counter for the free trial period.

All of this stays in the app's private storage on your phone. You can delete a single measurement, or all of a child's data, inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes the data from your phone.

If your phone's own backup is turned on (for example iCloud Backup or Google's Android backup), the app's data may be included in that backup. That backup is managed by Apple or Google under their own terms, not by us.

Camera, motion sensor, notifications

  • Camera: used only to take the height photo. The photo is processed on your phone.
  • Motion sensor: used only to show whether the phone is held upright. Nothing is recorded.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, the app schedules a weekly reminder on your phone. No notification service or server is involved.

You can turn any of these off in your phone's settings.

Purchases

The app offers a 14-day free trial that starts with your first measurement. After that, you can unlock Height Count Pro with a 3-month or yearly subscription (both renew automatically until canceled) or a one-time lifetime purchase. Purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details. The app asks the store whether you own the purchase or an active subscription so it can unlock itself.

Children

Height Count is meant for parents and guardians. It is not directed at children and children are not expected to use it. Information about a child (name, photo, height) is entered by the parent and stays on the parent's phone.

Not a medical device

Heights shown by the app are approximate estimates for your own records. The app is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. If you have concerns about your child's health or growth, please consult a doctor.
